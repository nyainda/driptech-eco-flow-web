## Common Questions and Answers

Q: How much does drip irrigation cost in Kenya?
A: Drip irrigation systems in Kenya cost between KES 35,000 to KES 150,000 per acre, depending on farm size, crop type, and system complexity. Small farm kits (0.25 acre) start from KES 15,000. DripTech offers free consultations to provide accurate quotes.

Q: How much water does drip irrigation save?
A: Drip irrigation saves 40-60% of water compared to traditional flood irrigation and 20-30% compared to sprinkler systems. On a 1-acre farm, this translates to savings of approximately 15,000-20,000 liters per week.

Q: What is the best irrigation system for small farms in Kenya?
A: For small farms (under 1 acre), drip irrigation is the most cost-effective and water-efficient option. DripTech offers starter kits specifically designed for small-scale farmers, with prices starting from KES 15,000.

Q: How long does irrigation installation take?
A: Installation time depends on farm size. Small farms (0.25-0.5 acre) take 1-2 days. Medium farms (1 acre) take 3-5 days. Large farms (5+ acres) take 1-2 weeks. This includes system design, installation, testing, and farmer training.

Q: Do you provide installation service?
A: Yes, all DripTech irrigation systems include professional installation service. Our certified technicians handle the complete installation, testing, and provide training on system operation and maintenance.

Q: What is the ROI for drip irrigation?
A: Most farmers recover their investment within 1-2 growing seasons through water savings (40-60% reduction in water costs) and increased yields (20-30% higher production). The system pays for itself and continues to provide savings for 10+ years.

Q: Which crops are suitable for drip irrigation?
A: Drip irrigation works for most crops including vegetables (tomatoes, cabbage, kale), fruits (strawberries, watermelon), flowers, tree crops (avocado, mango, citrus), and greenhouse crops. Different dripline spacing is used for different crops.

Q: Do you offer maintenance services?
A: Yes, DripTech provides comprehensive maintenance services including system inspection, filter cleaning, emitter replacement, leak repairs, and system upgrades. Maintenance visits cost KES 5,000-15,000 depending on system size.

Q: What warranty do you provide?
A: All DripTech irrigation equipment comes with a 5-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. Installation workmanship is guaranteed for 2 years. We also offer extended warranty options.

Q: Can I install the system myself?
A: While DIY installation is possible with our kits, we strongly recommend professional installation to ensure optimal performance and water efficiency. Professional installation is included in our system prices.
