# Irrigation Consultant Quotation – Detailed Schedule Template

Use this schedule for rain guns, drip tapes, rain hoses, hose options and pumps. Add selected products to the BOQ; put model specifications in each item Description and commercial answers in quotation Notes. The print/PDF HTML renderer labels the notes area **Technical Specifications, Commercial Terms & Notes** and preserves line breaks.

> Accuracy rule: populate pressure, flow, throw/radius, nozzle sizes, roll lengths, pressure ratings, pump duty points, prices and stock only from current supplier datasheets/product records or confirmed stock. A model family name is not a complete datasheet.

## A. Rain guns and spray guns
Requested models: PY30, PY40, PY50, FSN50, FSC50, FSW40, FS35 and FS40.

For each selected gun record: brand/exact model; inlet connection size and thread/flange type; main and auxiliary/spreader nozzle sizes and quantity; operating pressure (bar); flow at each nozzle/pressure (m³/h or L/min); wetted diameter/radius at stated pressure (m); rotation/adjustment characteristics; and whether gun head only or complete kit is quoted.

Accessories must be explicit: tripod, nozzle set, adaptor, clamps and seals each marked Included, Optional—priced separately, or Not included. Do not imply bundled accessories by default.

## C. Drip tape (0.30 mm and 0.40 mm)
For each SKU include wall thickness; nominal diameter; emitter spacing; emitter flow at stated test pressure; recommended and maximum operating pressure; filtration requirement; roll length; price per roll; and stock availability. Do not infer flow, pressure or roll length from wall thickness alone.

## D. Rain hose
Specify diameter/nominal bore; roll length; material/construction; working and burst pressure (if supplied); hole spacing/discharge pattern if perforated; connectors/clamps/end caps/repair pieces; price per roll; and minimum pack.

## E. Hose and pipe range
- **Layflat:** diameter, grade/reinforcement, working pressure, burst pressure, roll length.
- **Canvas:** diameter, lining/construction, coupling type, pressure rating, length.
- **Flexible PVC:** diameter, reinforced/non-reinforced, delivery or suction use, working pressure; vacuum rating for suction hose.
- **Micro-spray tube:** tube diameter, coil length, compatible emitters/sprayers, operating pressure.

Selection guidance: pressure-rated reinforced delivery hose for rain-gun pumping; purpose-made vacuum-rated suction hose for pump intake. Confirm diameter against flow and friction-loss calculations.

## F. Petrol and diesel pumps
For each pump provide brand/exact model; petrol/diesel engine, displacement and rated power; inlet/outlet sizes and connection type; pump curve or flow at stated head; total head, suction lift and duty point; fuel consumption, weight, warranty/service terms if available; and a clear nameplate photo or manufacturer datasheet.

Select a pump at the actual operating duty point (required gun flow and gun pressure, static elevation, suction lift and hose/pipe losses). Maximum flow or maximum head alone is not a suitable selection basis.

## G. Commercial information – answer all customer questions
Include MOQ by SKU/pack/roll; price basis (wholesale, retail or project/volume); VAT inclusive/exclusive and applicable rate; delivery location, lead time and charge; payment terms; quotation validity; stock status/procurement lead time; and whether accessories are included or separately priced.
